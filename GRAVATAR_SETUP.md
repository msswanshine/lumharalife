# Set Up Gravatar for hello@lumharalife.com

## Why Gravatar?

When you send emails from `hello@lumharalife.com` through Gmail's "Send mail as", the profile picture recipients see is often pulled from **Gravatar** (Globally Recognized Avatar), not your Google Account. Gravatar is used by Gmail, Outlook, and many other email services to display profile pictures.

## Step-by-Step Setup

### Step 1: Create a Gravatar Account

1. **Go to:** https://gravatar.com
2. **Click "Create Your Gravatar"** or **"Sign In"** (if you already have a WordPress.com account)
3. **Sign up** with your email:
   - Use `hello@lumharalife.com` as your email address
   - Create a password (or use WordPress.com if you have an account)

### Step 2: Verify Your Email

1. **Check your email** for a verification message from Gravatar
2. **Click the verification link** in the email
3. This confirms you own the email address

### Step 3: Upload Your Profile Picture

1. **After signing in**, you'll see "Add a new image"
2. **Click "Add a new image"**
3. **Choose upload method:**
   - **Upload new:** Select your headshot from your computer
   - **From URL:** If your headshot is already online
   - **From webcam:** Take a photo (not recommended for professional use)
4. **Select your headshot image** (`about-me_headshot.png` or similar)
5. **Crop/adjust** the image as needed
6. **Click "Crop Image"**

### Step 4: Rate Your Image

1. **Choose a rating:**
   - **G-rated:** Safe for all audiences (recommended for professional use)
   - **PG-rated:** Mild content
   - **R-rated:** Mature content
   - **X-rated:** Adult content
2. **Click "Set Rating"**

### Step 5: Assign Image to Email

1. **You'll see a list of your email addresses**
2. **Find `hello@lumharalife.com`**
3. **Click on the image** you just uploaded
4. **The image will be assigned** to that email address

### Step 6: Verify It's Working

1. **Go to:** https://gravatar.com/your-email-address
   - Replace `your-email-address` with `hello@lumharalife.com`
2. **You should see your headshot** displayed
3. **If it shows up here, it will show up in emails!**

## How It Works

- **Gravatar** is a service that provides profile pictures based on email addresses
- When someone receives an email from `hello@lumharalife.com`, their email client (Gmail, Outlook, etc.) looks up the Gravatar for that email
- If you've set up Gravatar, your headshot will appear automatically
- **No action needed by recipients** - it works automatically

## Troubleshooting

### Image Not Showing Up?

1. **Wait 5-10 minutes** - Gravatar caches can take time to update
2. **Clear recipient's cache:**
   - Ask them to clear their browser cache
   - Or wait 24 hours for caches to refresh
3. **Check Gravatar directly:**
   - Visit: `https://gravatar.com/hello@lumharalife.com`
   - If your image shows here, it's working correctly
4. **Verify email is correct:**
   - Make sure you verified `hello@lumharalife.com` in Gravatar
   - Check that the image is assigned to the correct email

### Multiple Email Addresses?

- You can add multiple emails to one Gravatar account
- Each email can have a different picture
- Or use the same picture for all emails

### Updating Your Picture Later

1. **Sign in to Gravatar**
2. **Go to "My Gravatars"**
3. **Click "Add a new image"** or edit existing
4. **Assign to `hello@lumharalife.com`**
5. **Changes appear within minutes to hours** (depending on caching)

## Alternative: Contact List Method

If Gravatar doesn't work for some recipients, they can manually add your picture:

1. **Recipient adds you to contacts** (Gmail, Outlook, etc.)
2. **They add your headshot** to the contact
3. **Your picture will show** in their email client

This only works per recipient, so Gravatar is the better solution for everyone.
