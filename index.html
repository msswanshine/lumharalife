About Amy

Hi, I'm Amy Swan.Experiential Director · Human Systems Strategist I help humans and teams understand how they're wired, and build something extraordinary with it.

Let's begin

I've been convening rooms and leading transformative conversations my whole life.

At seven, I wrote an opinion piece to keep my city's schools integrated. At a new school with no music teacher, I assembled strangers, handed out sheet music, and conducted... on day two. I built a safe space for LGBTQ+ youth in the 1990s before it was popular. I've been a musician, a rock 'n' roll stagehand, a software engineer at Apple and McKinsey, and a mentor who's created lasting change.

Every room. Every time. Something transforms.

I work at the intersection of Human Design and other Human Systems, live group experience, and 10+ years of tech expertise. Which means I can walk into any room — a startup, a nonprofit, a group of people searching for their next chapter — and speak everyone's language while transforming how they see themselves.

My work isn't about telling you who to be. It's about creating the conditions where you finally remember.
